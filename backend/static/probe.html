brownRB: pale → golden → brown axis. Should rise steadily as food browns, stay flat under lighting changes.
brownRG: secondary browning axis, same illumination-invariance as brownRB.
Yratio: centre brightness vs. surroundings - should barely move when you change the lights. This is the decisive on-device validation of the whole monitor design.
satC: centre colour saturation - tends to drop as food chars.
specC: fraction of blown/clipped centre pixels - rises with glare or steam highlights.
E: centre edge/texture energy - rises with bubbles or surface texture.
Acentre: centre frame-to-frame activity - rises when the food is visibly doing something.
boil: Acentre minus Aannulus - isolates food activity from someone nudging the pan or tablet.
referenceOk: false if the background/hob is too dark to serve as a lighting reference.
veiled: true = steam signature detected (high Yratio, low saturation, low texture).
